Windows 11 Rollback

After upgrading to Windows 11, you have 10 days to go back to your previous version of Windows if you're experiencing issues.

ℹ
Scenario: Rollback needed
In this scenario, you've upgraded to Windows 11 but discovered that a critical business application is not compatible. You need to roll back to Windows 10.

To initiate a rollback:

Step 1: Open Settings
Step 2: Go to System > Recovery
Step 3: Click "Go back" under Recovery options
Time limit: 10 days from upgrade

CompTIA A+ Exam Tip
The 10-day rollback window starts from the upgrade date. After this period, the Windows.old folder is automatically deleted to free up disk space. Always document upgrade dates and test critical applications immediately after an OS upgrade.
